# Market Research

The market has recruiting platforms, general transcription services, and ATS add-ons.
The open gap is a recruiting-specific assistant that writes structured notes and follow-up actions into the ATS.
The strongest entry point is a narrow tool for small agencies.

# Customer Research

The ideal customer is a US recruiting agency with 5 to 50 recruiters.
The agency owner buys the tool, and the coordinator feels the rework.
The main problems are inconsistent notes, lost follow-ups, and ATS retyping.
Pilot interest is the key buying trigger to test.

# GTM Strategy

The launch targets ten paid pilot agencies in one region.
The message leads with time saved per recruiter and ATS integration.
The sequence moves from direct outreach to case notes and a self-serve trial.
The goals are measurable time savings and one reference customer.

# Risks and Assumptions

- Assumption: agencies will accept a paid pilot within 90 days.
  Validation method: ask ten agency owners for a pilot commitment.
- Assumption: ATS vendors allow write access through a supported interface.
  Validation method: test the top three integrations in the pilot.
- Risk: candidate data rules differ by state.
  Mitigation: limit retention and document controls.
