# Ideal Customer Profile

- US recruiting agency with 5 to 50 recruiters.
- Places candidates in hourly, technical, or professional roles.
- Uses an ATS for candidates and job orders.
- Runs intake and screening calls on video or phone.

Interpretation: the best first buyer is an agency owner who also manages a small recruiter team.

# Personas

- Agency owner: buys tools, controls budget, and cares about recruiter output.
- Senior recruiter: runs high call volume and owns candidate relationships.
- Recruiting coordinator: cleans notes and moves candidates between stages.

# Customer Problems

- Notes are inconsistent across recruiters.
- Follow-up actions are lost after a call.
- Retyping notes into the ATS takes time.
- Candidate requirements are hard to compare across roles.

Interpretation: the coordinator feels the rework, and the owner sees the cost.

# Buying Triggers

- New client volume exceeds current staff capacity.
- A recruiter leaves and notes are incomplete.
- The agency adopts a new ATS and wants clean data.
- A client asks for faster candidate summaries.

# Assumptions and Open Questions

- Assumption: agencies will accept a paid pilot within 90 days.
  Validation method: ask ten agency owners for a pilot commitment.
  Impact if wrong: the launch needs a longer sales cycle.
- Assumption: ATS vendors allow write access through a supported interface.
  Validation method: test the top three ATS integrations in the pilot.
  Impact if wrong: the product starts with export and copy only.
- Open question: what retention period do agencies require for call recordings?
- Open question: who approves the use of candidate data in each agency?
